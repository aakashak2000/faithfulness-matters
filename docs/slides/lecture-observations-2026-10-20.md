# What the 2026-10-20 presentation session showed (24 teams, recording captions)
Source: `CSCI 544 - Ren Lecture Recording on 10_20_2026_Captions_English (United States).txt` (local, Downloads). Questions were mostly from the professor; peers fill the same rubric on a Google form, one submission per team.

## Format as run
- 2 minutes to speak, a timer, "we will cut you off at the end of two minutes"; then 1 minute of questions; about 1 minute of transition. 24 teams a day. The next batch is Thursday.
- Slides sit in a shared deck under each team's index; presenters use a mic and were asked to speak close to it (the room and a Zoom audience could not hear several speakers).
- Closing advice from the professor: "don't try to put too many things on the content ... it doesn't really help people to follow ... control the times, stick with whatever you can do in two minutes."
- Scope advice given to two teams: reduce the number of variables and focus on clearing out results.

## What this means for us
See the new rows 17 to 24 in `qa.md`, and the changes to the script and slides noted in the session: the 2-minute cut-off is hard, the task and output must be stated explicitly, the chart needs a one-line "how to read it", and prior work should include recent (2025, 2026) papers.
