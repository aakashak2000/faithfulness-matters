## Positioning (conservative)
Gated-fusion emotion models motivate the question (their gates are natural to read as modality importance, but the abstracts report accuracy). Hessel and Lee and Jin et al. are precedents for intervention and saliency analyses of modalities, for different questions (cross-modal interactions; distillation weights). The attention-explanation papers show why a weight cannot be assumed to be an explanation. Our contribution is a focused empirical audit: for utterance-level text+audio emotion models, how often does the original gate weight predict which modality a swap moves more, relative to a trivial constant rule, and when does that advantage disappear. It is not a new theory of causality, not a new swap method, and not evidence that every gated system fails.
